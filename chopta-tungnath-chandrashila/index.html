WHO WE ARE

About Trip Trek India

At Trip Trek India, we turn travel dreams into unforgettable journeys. Specializing in domestic group departures and customized trips across India, we design experiences that combine adventure, comfort, and culture. With Trip Trek India, you don't just visit places — you live the journey.

This beginner-friendly trek takes you to Tungnath, the highest Shiva temple in the world, up to the Chandrashila summit at 13,549 ft, and down to the peaceful Deoriatal Lake — all in a short, well-paced weekend window.

WHAT AWAITS YOU

Major Trip Highlights

Trek to Tungnath — the highest Shiva temple in the world.

Summit Chandrashila — 13,549 ft, for 360° Himalayan views.

Visit Devprayag Sangam & Dhari Devi Temple en route.

Scenic stay in Sari Village — homestay or campsite.

Peaceful trek to Deoriatal Lake — mirror-still Himalayan reflections.

Bonfire & group activities — evenings with fellow trekkers.

Beginner-friendly and short — a genuine summit trek in a single weekend.

DAY BY DAY

Your Itinerary

  • Reporting: 9:30–10:30 PM at Akshardham Metro Station, Delhi.
  • Travel: Overnight travel with music & games onboard.
  • Halt: Tea/dinner halt en route (self-paid).
  • Arrival: Early arrival at Rishikesh.
  • Sightseeing: Visit Devprayag Sangam and Dhari Devi Temple.
  • Check-In: Check in at Sari.
  • Evening: Explore the village, bonfire & group activities.
  • Overnight Stay: Homestay / campsite.
  • Drive: Drive to Chopta base (via Baniyakund).
  • Darshan: Darshan at Tungnath Temple.
  • Summit: Climb to Chandrashila summit (360° Himalayan view).
  • Return: Return to base.
  • Overnight Stay: Dinner & rest at Sari.
  • Check-Out: After breakfast, check out from the hotel.
  • Trek: Morning trek to Deoriatal.
  • At the Lake: Free time by the lake for reflection & photography.
  • Return: Return by noon and depart for Delhi.
  • Optional: Omkareshwar Temple visit.
  • Evening: Reach Rishikesh by evening.
  • Travel: Overnight journey to Delhi.

Inclusions & Exclusions

Included in your package

  • Pickup & drop from Delhi / Rishikesh
  • Accommodation (homestay / campsite) on sharing basis
  • Meals: Day 1 dinner, Day 2 breakfast & dinner, Day 3 breakfast
  • Guided treks (Tungnath, Chandrashila, Deoriatal)
  • Bonfire & group activities

Not included in your package

  • Meals during travel (Delhi to Rishikesh route)
  • Personal snacks, beverages & bottled water
  • Trek gear (jackets, shoes, raincoats, poles, etc.)
  • Forest entry fees (Deoriatal, Chandrashila, etc.)
  • 4x4 / shelter vehicle (if required from Baniyakund)
  • Travel / health insurance
  • Personal expenses (laundry, souvenirs, etc.)
  • Optional excursions not in the itinerary
  • Tips for guides / drivers
