THE TRIP

About the Darma Valley Trip

Darma Valley lies in the far east of Uttarakhand, in Pithoragarh district, close to the Nepal border and below the Panchachuli peaks. It is a remote valley of river gorges and small villages, reached by a long mountain drive from Delhi.

The trip passes the Chitai Golu Temple and Koli Dhek Lake, spends a night at Dharchula, then goes on to the Nepal border area and Darma Valley for a campfire evening. The next day you walk towards Panchachuli Base Camp, then drive on to Pithoragarh before the journey back to Delhi.

WHAT AWAITS YOU

Major Trip Highlights

Chitai Golu Temple — a Champawat temple known for its many bells.

Koli Dhek Lake — a lake stop near Lohaghat, subject to access.

Dharchula — a night at the gateway to the remote valleys.

Nepal border area — a visit to the accessible border near Dharchula, subject to local rules.

Darma Valley — a drive through Tawaghat and Sobla into the valley.

Dugtu and Dantu — mountain villages and river views.

Campfire and music — an evening with the group (subject to conditions).

Panchachuli Base Camp — a walk towards the base camp route, with Zero Point if conditions allow.

DAY BY DAY

Your Itinerary

  • Departure: Depart from Delhi at night. Your pickup point and time are shared once your booking is confirmed.
  • Meet the group: Meet your fellow travellers and board the vehicle for the overnight journey towards Uttarakhand.
  • On the way: Refreshment and comfort breaks are planned along the route.
  • Overnight journey: Travel overnight towards Dharchula.
  • Morning: Continue through the scenic Kumaon hills.
  • Champawat: Visit the Chitai Golu Temple in Champawat, known for the many bells offered by devotees.
  • Lohaghat: Drive on to Lohaghat and visit Koli Dhek Lake (subject to access and conditions).
  • Meals on the way: Stops for breakfast, refreshments and lunch (lunch is not included).
  • Drive: Continue towards Dharchula, the gateway to the remote Himalayan valleys.
  • Night: Reach Dharchula by evening, depending on road conditions. Check in, have dinner and rest.
  • Morning: Wake up early, have breakfast and get ready for the day.
  • Nepal border: Visit the accessible Nepal border area near Dharchula, subject to local rules and permissions.
  • Darma Valley: Continue to Darma Valley via Tawaghat and Sobla, with mountain landscapes, river valleys and remote settlements on the way.
  • Dugtu and Dantu: Travel towards the Dugtu and Dantu region, depending on road access and conditions.
  • Check-in: Check in at the homestay and relax in the mountains.
  • Evening: Campfire and music with the group (subject to weather, local rules and fire-safety conditions), then dinner.
  • Night: Overnight stay in Darma Valley.
  • Morning: Wake up early and have breakfast.
  • Walk: Walk towards the accessible Panchachuli Base Camp route or designated viewpoints, following local guidance.
  • Views: See the Panchachuli peaks, weather and visibility permitting.
  • Zero Point: If trail conditions, weather, daylight, fitness and local permissions allow, you may continue towards Zero Point.
  • Drive back: Return to the vehicle and drive back to Dharchula, then on to Pithoragarh.
  • Night: Check in at Pithoragarh, have dinner and rest.
  • Please note: This is the most demanding day. The walk and the Zero Point extension are not guaranteed, and the plan may be shortened for safe trekking and driving.
  • Morning: Wake up early, check out and begin the drive to Delhi.
  • On the way: Drive through the Kumaon region with planned meal and comfort breaks.
  • Arrival: Reach Delhi by night, subject to road and traffic conditions.
  • Trip ends: The trip ends with new friends and a lot of mountain memories.
