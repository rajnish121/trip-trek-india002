ABOUT US

Travel that is easy, fun and never hectic

Trip Trek India is a Delhi-based travel company that plans Himalayan treks, weekend getaways and group trips, so you can just show up and enjoy every moment.

FOUNDER
Rajnish Yadav

Rajnish is a hardcore traveller who started Trip Trek India with one belief: the mountains and the road belong to everyone, not only to people with the time, the contacts or the experience to plan a trip alone.

Years of travelling taught him how much of a trip is planning, and how much stress that planning can add. Trip Trek India takes that part off your shoulders. The transport, the stay, the meals and the route are sorted, and an experienced Trip Captain stays with the group the whole way.

He still shapes the trips himself, so every itinerary reflects what a traveller would actually want from the journey.

CO-FOUNDER
Aman Singh

Aman believes a trip should feel like a holiday from the first message to the last photo. He looks after bookings and guest support, so your questions get a real answer and the trip you book is the trip you get.

His idea of a good journey is simple: no rushing, no stress, and plenty of laughs along the way. Travel is meant to be enjoyed, not survived.

OUR VISION

Make travel easy, accessible and fun for everyone

We want anyone who dreams of a trip to the mountains to be able to take it, whether it is a first trek, a solo journey, a family getaway or a group of friends. And once you are on the road, we want you to enjoy every moment of it, without the rush and the stress.

EasyOne clear package with stay, transport, meals and a Trip Captain. Book with a small advance and we handle the rest.
AccessibleFair, transparent prices and trips for different budgets and experience levels, from weekend getaways to pilgrim treks.
Fun, never hecticRelaxed plans, sensible pacing and small groups where strangers become friends, so every moment is enjoyable.
