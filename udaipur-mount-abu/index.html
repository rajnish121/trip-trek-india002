THE DESTINATION

About Udaipur & Mount Abu

Udaipur is Rajasthan at its most romantic: shimmering lakes, grand palaces and lanes full of colour. Over two days you will walk through the gardens of Saheliyon Ki Bari, watch the sun settle over Fateh Sagar Lake, explore the City Palace and glide across Lake Pichola by boat.

Then it is time for a change of scenery: a scenic drive to Mount Abu, Rajasthan's only hill station, for Nakki Lake, Toad Rock, a stroll down Mall Road and the marble artistry of the Dilwara Temples. Your Trip Captain looks after the details while you enjoy the journey with a friendly group.

WHAT AWAITS YOU

Major Trip Highlights

Saheliyon Ki Bari — the lush gardens and fountains.

Fateh Sagar Lake — lakeside views, the Aquarium and the Maharana Pratap Memorial.

Fateh Sagar Market — street food and an easy, lively evening.

City Palace — a grand symbol of Rajput heritage.

Lake Pichola boat ride — Udaipur from the water.

Jagdish Temple & Karni Mata Ropeway — blessings, then panoramic city views.

Gangaur Ghat — with a folk performance at Bagore Ki Haveli or time in the local markets.

Mount Abu — Nakki Lake, Toad Rock, Mall Road and the marble Dilwara Temples.

Music & bonfire evening — subject to weather conditions.

DAY BY DAY

Your Itinerary

  • Assembly: Meet at Akshardham Metro Station between 9:30 and 10:30 PM.
  • Meet & Greet: Say hello to your Trip Captain and fellow travellers.
  • Departure: Board the vehicle and set off towards Udaipur.
  • On the Way: Get to know each other with introductions, music and group games.
  • Dinner Halt: Stop at a roadside dhaba for refreshments and dinner (self-paid).
  • Travel: Overnight journey to Udaipur.
  • Arrival: Reach Udaipur and check in at the hotel.
  • Saheliyon Ki Bari: Wander through the lush gardens and fountains.
  • Fateh Sagar Lake: Enjoy the lakeside views.
  • Sights: Visit the Aquarium and the Maharana Pratap Memorial.
  • Evening: Fateh Sagar Market for local street food and a lively atmosphere.
  • Night: Dinner at the hotel with a music evening, then overnight stay in Udaipur.
  • Morning: Breakfast, then head out for a full-day sightseeing tour.
  • City Palace: Explore this grand symbol of Rajput heritage.
  • Lake Pichola: Enjoy a boat ride on the lake.
  • Jagdish Temple: Seek blessings at the historic temple.
  • Ropeway: Ride the Karni Mata Ropeway for panoramic views of the city.
  • Evening: Gangaur Ghat, then a folk performance at Bagore Ki Haveli or time in the local markets.
  • Night: Return to the hotel for dinner and an overnight stay.
  • Morning: Breakfast and hotel check-out.
  • Drive: Head to Mount Abu, about 165 km from Udaipur.
  • Nakki Lake & Toad Rock: Visit two of the hill station's best-known spots.
  • Mall Road: Stroll through the busy market street.
  • Dilwara Temples: See the famous temples known for their intricate marble carving.
  • Leisure: Free time to explore Mount Abu at your own pace.
  • Return: Begin the journey back to Delhi in the evening.
