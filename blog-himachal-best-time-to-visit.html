When to Visit Himachal Pradesh: A Season-by-Season Guide

Snow, monsoon greens, or crisp autumn skies — Himachal looks completely different depending on when you go.

Summer (March – June): Escape the Plains Heat

As temperatures climb across Delhi and the plains, Himachal stays pleasantly cool — daytime temperatures in Manali and McLeodganj typically sit between 15–25°C. Late spring also brings clear mountain views before the haze of peak summer sets in. This is peak season for a reason: expect busier roads and markets, especially around Manali.

Monsoon (July – August): Green, Quiet, and Best Avoided for High Passes

The monsoon transforms the lower valleys — Jibhi and the Tirthan Valley turn a deep, waterfall-fed green that's hard to see any other time of year. That said, this is also when landslides are most likely on mountain roads, and high-altitude routes like the Atal Tunnel and Rohtang can see disruptions. If you go, build flexibility into your plans.

Autumn (September – November): The Clearest Skies

Many regulars consider autumn the best season in Himachal. The monsoon clouds clear out, humidity drops, and visibility on treks like Triund and in the Kasol–Manikaran belt is often at its best. Days are still comfortably warm, while nights start to turn crisp — pack a warm layer even if daytime feels mild.

Winter (December – February): Snow, If That's What You Want

Manali and Solang Valley turn into a snow-sports destination in winter, with fresh snowfall through December and January. McLeodganj gets noticeably colder and quieter, which some travellers prefer for a peaceful trip. Road conditions can be affected by snowfall, so winter trips are best booked with some flexibility around departure dates.

Matching Season to Trip

  • Want snow? Manali – Kasol, December to February.
  • Want waterfalls and green valleys? Jibhi Tirthan Valley, July to September.
  • Want the clearest mountain views? McLeodganj – Triund, October to November.
  • Want warm days without the summer crowds? Any of our Himachal trips, late April to early June.
